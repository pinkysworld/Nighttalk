Privacy is a product feature.

NightTalk has no publisher-operated backend, no advertising SDK, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDK. The website also has no analytics or contact form.

Privacy details

Record responsibly.

Only use NightTalk where everyone who may be captured has given permission. Recording laws vary by location. NightTalk detects possible voice events; it cannot confirm sleep talking and is not medical advice.

NightTalk is a personal reflection and wellness app. It does not diagnose, monitor, prevent, or treat medical conditions.
