Personal by design

Your nights are not our data.

Recordings, transcripts, and imported sleep summaries are stored locally and excluded from iCloud Backup. Optional HealthKit access is read-only. Support cannot retrieve deleted nights.

Read the privacy policy

Curiosity, with clear limits.

NightTalk detects possible voice events. It may miss speech or retain other sounds; it cannot confirm sleep talking or diagnose a condition. Only record with permission from everyone who may be captured. Never rely on the app for medical, safety, or emergency decisions.
