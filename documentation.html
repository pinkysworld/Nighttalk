User guide

Your first night, explained.

For NightTalk on iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

1. Prepare your device

Complete onboarding and obtain permission from everyone who might be recorded. Place your device securely near your bed with its microphone unobstructed. Keep it ventilated and use a charger for long sessions; do not put it under bedding.

2. Start and stop a night

Tap Start Night and allow microphone access. The recording screen shows the session status and microphone activity. Recording can continue with the screen locked. In the morning, tap Stop and Create Summary and allow local analysis to finish.

If recording is interrupted, the screen shows a paused state. Follow the displayed instructions; you can stop the session safely and start a new night when ready. NightTalk limits saved candidate clips to protect storage and displays a warning when that limit is reached.

3. Review your results

Open the morning summary or select a night in History. Review possible voice events and play retained clips. Detection can miss speech or retain other sounds. A quiet or empty summary does not prove that no sleep talking occurred.

Optional transcription

With Pro, enable On-device Transcription in Settings and choose a supported language. Speech permission is optional. Recognition availability depends on your device, language, and system state; an unavailable transcript does not prevent audio recording or playback.

Optional HealthKit sleep context

Enable HealthKit Sleep Import in Settings and choose whether to share sleep data. NightTalk reads overlapping sleep samples and never writes Health data. No results may appear if access is denied or samples are not yet available. Recording works with this option off.

Free nights and Pro

Three completed sessions with at least 60 minutes of active capture are included. Audio interruptions do not count toward that threshold. Pro provides unlimited nights and optional transcripts and derived insights. Purchase prices and applicable trial terms appear before payment.

Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Use Manage Subscription to manage renewal. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted recordings.

Retention and deletion

Choose a retention period in Settings. Shortening it asks for confirmation before removing older nights. Delete All NightTalk Data permanently removes night summaries, recordings, transcripts, and imported sleep summaries. Preferences and the free-night counter remain. Local content is excluded from iCloud Backup; deleted or uninstalled content cannot be recovered by support.

Troubleshooting

  • Microphone unavailable: check NightTalk’s microphone permission in system Settings and disconnect an unexpected audio route.
  • Plans do not load: check your connection and use Try Again. Apple services are needed for purchases.
  • No HealthKit results: check shared sleep data and the recorded time window.
  • Storage warning: stop and review the night, then remove unwanted recordings or shorten retention.
