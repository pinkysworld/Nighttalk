Before you write

NightTalk stores recordings and insights locally. It cannot recover deleted nights, access your device’s recordings remotely, or restore local data after an uninstall.

Common questions

How do I start a night?

Complete onboarding, choose Start Night, and allow Microphone access. NightTalk shows a persistent recording status while capture is active.

Why are there no clips?

NightTalk saves a bounded number of short candidate clips, then filters them locally. It can miss events or include false positives; it does not promise medical or diagnostic detection.

How do I delete my data?

In NightTalk, go to Settings and choose Delete All NightTalk Data. This permanently removes local night content; Restore Purchases does not restore deleted nights.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings, choose Upgrade to NightTalk Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Apple Account eligibility determines what can be restored.

Can I manage a subscription?

Use the Manage Subscription option in NightTalk settings or open your Apple Account subscription settings. A lifetime purchase does not renew automatically.

How does HealthKit work?

HealthKit sleep import is optional and read-only. NightTalk remains usable when HealthKit is off, denied, or has no overlapping sleep samples.

Recording consent matters.

Use NightTalk only where everyone who may be captured has given permission. Recording laws vary by location.
